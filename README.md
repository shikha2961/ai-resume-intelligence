# AI Resume Intelligence
AI Resume Intelligence is a powerful tool designed to help job seekers optimize their resumes for better visibility and higher chances of landing interviews. By leveraging advanced AI algorithms, this tool analyzes your resume and provides personalized recommendations to enhance its effectiveness.

___

## Features
- **Resume Analysis**: The tool scans your resume for key elements such as keywords, formatting, and overall structure to identify areas for improvement.
- **Personalized Recommendations**: Based on the analysis, it offers tailored suggestions to enhance your resume's content and presentation.
- **Keyword Optimization**: It identifies relevant keywords that are commonly used in job descriptions and suggests incorporating them into your resume to increase its chances of passing through Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).
- **Formatting Tips**: The tool provides guidance on how to format your resume for better readability and professionalism, including font choices, layout, and section organization.
